Our story

Why CallMeNow exists

Indian streets move fast — parking, bumps, headlights left on. The fix is usually a quick chat, not a fight. We built a way to be reachable on your terms, without pasting your personal number on the glass.

The problem we saw

Parking chaos, blocked lanes, and small incidents happen every day. The honest fix is often a 30-second conversation — but nobody wants their mobile number stuck where strangers, scrapers, and cameras can grab it.

CallMeNow fills that gap: a QR sticker that opens a fast scan page — you choose how people can reach you, without broadcasting your digits.
